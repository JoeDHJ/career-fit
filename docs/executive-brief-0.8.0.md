# Career Fit: an evidence-to-decision work sample

Career Fit connects employer research, reviewed candidate evidence, assessed learning and constrained workforce planning. The project demonstrates analytical judgment through visible assumptions and reproducible outputs.

The source research contains eight attributed official employer descriptions and five learning resources. SQLite queries summarize this defined sample, preserving seniority gates, source access state and missing provider durations. Reporting is coded in all eight descriptions, SQL in six and Excel in four. These are sample counts, not market estimates.

An archived NHS analyst learning case selects Excel, SQL, a workforce memo and a reconciliation task in 46 hours, including preparation. Content/practice fees are zero; software and equipment are excluded. At an assumed 20 USD time value, economic cost is 920 USD. A four-part task rubric and explicit re-review connect a completed artifact to the evidence map. Existing learning history survives the round trip.

The medium workforce case compares 30 synthetic employees against 12 positions. The hybrid plan fills 12 positions and 24 priority units with 21,800 USD cash, 81,040 USD incremental economic cost and 88 training hours. The no-training alternative fills 10 positions and 18 units; different coverage prevents a like-for-like savings claim. Learning-effect and backfill sensitivity can overturn the full-coverage result.

External SkillSpan evaluation exposes limited exact extraction accuracy. The product consequently treats extraction as review suggestions and keeps capability, hiring probability and training effects outside the model's validated claims. No human user study or real employee outcome is reported.
