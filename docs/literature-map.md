# Literature to product map

Career Fit uses the literature as a design guide for clearer job-search decisions. The papers do not validate the product's scores, and the product does not claim to estimate ability, productivity, wages, or hiring probability.

| Research idea | Applied in Career Fit | User value | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Deming and Kahn, 2018](https://www.nber.org/papers/w23328) study changing skill requirements and the mix of cognitive and social demands in job postings. | The Role Fingerprint separates categories such as cognitive, social, and software skills, while the requirement matrix keeps every named skill visible. | A user can see whether the main preparation issue is analytical proof, tool evidence, or communication translation. | A posting-level category is not a measure of a person's capability or an employer's production function. |
| [Deming, 2017](https://academic.oup.com/qje/article-abstract/132/4/1593/3861633?login=false) documents the growing labor-market importance of social skills. | Stakeholder communication and related social requirements are treated as explicit evidence targets and can appear in integrated proof bundles. | Users get a concrete way to translate research or technical work into audience, decision, and result. | Career Fit does not convert a social-skill label into a wage premium or hiring advantage. |
| [Guvenen, Kuruscu, Tanaka, and Wiczer, 2020](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/mac.20160241) develop a multidimensional view of skill mismatch. | Category coverage and mismatch dimensions summarize several types of role evidence instead of collapsing everything into one keyword score. | A job seeker can prioritize the largest evidence dimension without losing the underlying skill-level audit trail. | The coverage formula is a transparent preparation index, not an estimated mismatch distribution for the economy. |
| [Postel-Vinay and Lise, 2022](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.20162002) model multidimensional skills and occupational transitions. | Transferable evidence stays discounted and is turned into a bridge action rather than silently upgraded to direct equivalence. | Career switchers see what to verify or demonstrate next. | The product does not estimate transition probabilities or worker-specific returns. |
| [Gathmann and Schonberg, 2010](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/full/10.1086/649786) and [Poletaev and Robinson, 2008](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.1086/588180) motivate task-sensitive views of occupational mobility and human-capital specificity. | Gap actions distinguish translation, bridge, foundation, and verification needs, and skill bundles are expressed as proof-artifact ideas. | A user can build one reviewable work sample that combines related requirements. | The bundle is co-occurrence within the supplied posting. It is not a market complementarity or wage estimate. |

## Design rule

The practical rule is simple: research can motivate what to display and what to keep separate, but it cannot turn a sparse job posting and a self-described profile into a hiring forecast. The interface therefore shows evidence, uncertainty, and a next action together.
