# Two-minute demonstration

Start `career-fit serve` and visit `http://127.0.0.1:8766/capital`.

**0:00–0:25 — The question.** “A person has limited money and time. An employer has limited training capacity. Career Fit turns reviewed skill evidence into feasible investment choices.” Point to the fictional-person label and the real-JD source. Expand the inputs to show that the candidate, effort, costs and training outcomes are supplied assumptions.

**0:25–0:55 — Personal decision.** Review the catalog, confirm the inputs and compare. Show $320 cash, 68 hours and $1,680 including time. Explain the proposed artifacts. Point to the two unmet experience gates: “A course cannot create seven years of recruiting experience.” Reduce cash to $150 and recalculate to show a smaller feasible portfolio. Returns remain absent unless a benefit basis is entered.

**0:55–1:30 — Organization decision.** Select Workforce allocation, review and compare. Show the four strategy rows, two direct moves and two proposed training moves. Explain $1,400 fees plus $1,616 released time. The no-training baseline fills three positions; avoid claiming an equal-coverage saving. Change cash to $30,000 to compare the same four positions and expose the $11,984 scenario saving.

**1:30–1:50 — Evidence and limits.** Show named skill gaps, eligibility and source vintages. “O*NET supplies occupational importance, not employee competency thresholds. BLS supplies national estimates and projections, not individual training returns.” Point out the national-only geographic coverage where applicable.

**1:50–2:00 — Delivery.** Export Markdown or JSON. Show the repository's reproducible cases and methods. “The same reviewed input drives the screen and report. Installation, calculation and browser checks support this result.” Return to Job seeker workspace to demonstrate the original resume-free review and Markdown/PDF workflow if time permits.
