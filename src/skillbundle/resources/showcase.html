MEASUREMENT · OUTSIDE THE DEMO

        
A useful workflow must expose weak extraction.

        

          SkillSpan test results show limited exact-span accuracy. These
          extractors generate review suggestions; they do not measure capability
          or predict hiring. Skill-only and combined skill/knowledge targets
          answer different questions.
        

        

        

        SkillSpan paper and dataset ↗
        

          What the metrics mean
          

            Exact F1 requires both span boundaries to match. Overlap F1 allows
            intersecting boundaries with maximum one-to-one matching. Micro
            scores pool all spans, including negative sentences. Neither metric
            validates downstream fit weights or training effects.
